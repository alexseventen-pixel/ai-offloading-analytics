Student AI-Use & Cognitive Offloading Analytics

Decision-support tool for teachers and school leadership of a Latvian international school (grades 8–9). It operationalises the Master's thesis “Use of AI Tools and Cognitive Offloading among 8th and 9th Grade Students: Associations with Attention and Critical Thinking in a Latvian International School”: the 12-item AI-use questionnaire, the AI Offloading Composite Score (Q4, Q8, Q9, Q10r, Q11r, Q12r), Luria's 10 Words Test, Schulte Tables, Digit Span and the Critical Thinking “Survival Story” task.

Research hypothesis (thesis): higher AI-based cognitive offloading is associated with lower attention and critical thinking scores.
