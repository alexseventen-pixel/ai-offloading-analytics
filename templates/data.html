Data & pipeline

Pipeline

  1. Generation – generate_dataset.py simulates {{ n_raw }} students (seed 2026) using the thesis instruments; ~3% missing answers injected.
  2. Load – CSV → SQLite table students (db.init_db); currently {{ n_db }} rows in DB.
  3. Derivation – offloading composite (Q4, Q8, Q9, 6−Q10, 6−Q11, 6−Q12; computed if ≥ 4 of 6 items present), Low/Higher AI-use group (Q4 split), CT total, attention index, risk flags.
  4. Cleaning for ML – median imputation (numeric), most-frequent imputation (categorical), z-scaling, one-hot encoding inside a scikit-learn Pipeline (no leakage).
  5. Analysis – Pearson/Spearman correlations, Welch t-test, Cohen's d (analysis.py); classifiers (ml.py).
